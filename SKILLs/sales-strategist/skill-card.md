## Description:

销售幕后军师（sales-strategist）is a Chinese-language sales coaching skill that analyzes customer conversations from screenshots, voice transcripts, or text and produces intention decoding, customer profiles, conversion-focused response scripts, and follow-up strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales teams and individual sellers use this skill to interpret Chinese-language customer conversations, handle objections, identify vague commitments, and draft follow-up messages that can be sent to customers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks users to provide customer chats, screenshots, voice content, or call transcripts that may contain personal data, account details, pricing, contracts, or confidential business information.

Mitigation: Redact names, phone numbers, account details, pricing, contracts, and other confidential information before use, and only upload recordings or third-party conversations with proper consent or authorization.

Risk: Generated sales advice and customer-intent interpretations may be incorrect or too aggressive for the relationship, product, or legal context.

Mitigation: Treat the analysis and scripts as draft guidance, review them before sending to customers, and adjust for accuracy, consent, applicable sales rules, and relationship risk.
