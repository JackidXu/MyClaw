# 卖点-买点翻译器

## Purpose

Guide any business through a structured 5-step process to extract selling points from
internal product/business language and translate them into customer buying points —
language that resonates with what the customer actually cares about.

Core principle: **卖点是"我有什么"，买点是"你能怎样"** (Selling points = what I have;
buying points = what you gain). Most businesses fail because they keep talking in
internal language (features, specs) instead of customer language (outcomes, risk relief).

## When to Use

Use this skill when the user:
- Asks to extract or refine business selling points (提炼卖点/购买理由/买点)
- Wants to translate product features into customer-facing messaging
- Needs help crafting value propositions or sales copy
- Says their product is "hard to explain" or "customers don't get it"
- Wants to differentiate from competitors through messaging

## The 5-Step Process

Guide the user through each step sequentially. Do not skip steps. At each step,
ask the user specific questions (listed below) and wait for their answers before
proceeding to the next step.

### Step 1: Business DNA Scan (业务DNA扫描)

Goal: Inventory everything the business could potentially claim as a selling point.
Don't filter yet — just collect raw material.

Ask the user to describe their business across 4 dimensions:

1. **Product/Service itself**: Functions, specs, form factor, usage flow, delivery method.
   Ask: "你的产品/服务具体是什么？怎么用的？有哪些功能模块？"

2. **Technical/Professional advantages**: Patents, algorithms, data, processes, team expertise,
   exclusive resources.
   Ask: "你们有什么别人没有的技术、方法、资源或经验积累？"

3. **Business model differences**: Pricing structure, service model, response speed,
   after-sales, channels, supply chain.
   Ask: "在价格、服务方式、渠道、售后上，你们跟别人有什么不同？"

4. **Organizational trust proofs**: Founder background, customer cases, certifications,
   awards, funding, partners.
   Ask: "你有什么资质、案例、客户、数据或背书可以证明你的实力？"

### Step 2: Customer Job Map (客户任务地图)

Goal: Understand what job the customer is "hiring" the product to do. Use the
Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD) framework across three layers.

Ask the user:

1. **Functional job**: What specific problem does the customer need solved?
   "客户买你，是为了完成什么具体任务？不买你会用什么替代方案（竞品、自己动手、忍着）？"

2. **Emotional job**: How does the customer want to feel?
   "客户希望在使用你的产品/服务后，获得什么样的感受？（安心、掌控、自信、被尊重...）"

3. **Social job**: How does the customer want to be perceived by others?
   "客户希望因为用了你的产品/服务，在别人眼里成为什么样的人？"

Also ask about risks the customer fears most when making this purchase:
"客户在做购买决策时，最怕踩什么坑、冒什么风险？"

### Step 3: Value Pyramid (价值金字塔提炼)

Goal: Organize raw selling points into a hierarchical pyramid from concrete to
abstract. Guide the user to fill each layer:

```
价值层 ← Deep need satisfied (freedom, dignity, security, identity)
利益层 ← Concrete outcome delivered (save time, save money, earn more, avoid risk, improve health)
优势层 ← Competitive edge (faster, more stable, cheaper, more experienced)
属性层 ← Raw attributes (features, specs, appearance)
```

For each layer, ask:
- 属性层: "你的产品具体是什么、有什么功能？"
- 优势层: "它比别的方案好在哪里？为什么更好？"
- 利益层: "这个'更好'给客户带来什么实际结果？（省时/省钱/赚钱/避险）"
- 价值层: "这个结果满足客户的什么深层需求？（自由/尊严/安全感/身份认同）"

**Critical rule**: The top two layers (利益层 and 价值层) are the real selling points.
属性层 and 优势层 are just supporting evidence — they should never be the main message.

### Step 4: Buy-Point Translation (买点翻译)

Goal: Convert every selling point from seller language to buyer language using
the "那又怎样？" (So what?) test. Keep asking "so what?" until reaching an emotional
or practical outcome the customer cares about.

Learn the translation pattern from `references/examples.md` for cross-industry examples.

**Translation formula**:

> 当客户【处在某场景】时，通过我的【某种能力】，他能获得【具体结果】，
> 从而【生活/工作变成什么样】，并且不用担心【风险点】

**Translation pairs to practice with the user**:

| Seller Language (卖点) | → | Buyer Language (买点) |
|---|---|---|
| We use AI algorithms | → | You don't need to manually sift through data — get conclusions in 5 min |
| 10 years of industry experience | → | You won't repeat mistakes others have already made |
| 24/7 customer service | → | Someone has your back even at 2 AM |
| 30% cheaper than competitors | → | Same budget, one extra channel to test |
| Served Fortune 500 companies | → | Your boss and colleagues won't question your vendor choice |

For each selling point the user provides, apply the "那又怎样？" test repeatedly
until you reach an outcome the customer directly experiences. Present the result
to the user for confirmation.

### Step 5: Validation & Ranking (验证与排序)

Goal: Score and prioritize all translated buying points to identify the strongest
messaging to lead with.

Score each buying point on 4 dimensions (1-5 scale):

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| 重要性 (Importance) | How much does the customer care about this? |
| 独特性 (Uniqueness) | Can only you claim this, or can everyone? |
| 可信度 (Credibility) | Do you have evidence, data, cases to back it up? |
| 传播性 (Spreadability) | Can it be explained in one sentence? Will customers repeat it? |

Present a scored table to the user. Recommend leading with high-scoring points
on the homepage, video hooks, and sales opening lines.

## Key Translation Principles (reiterate throughout)

1. **Never lead with features.** Features are evidence, not selling points.
2. **The "那又怎样？" test is law.** Keep asking until you hit a customer outcome.
3. **One purchase reason per customer type.** If you have multiple target customers,
   run the process separately for each.
4. **Risk reversal is buying power.** Eliminating a fear is often stronger than
   promising a gain.
5. **Specific > General.** "Save 3 hours per week" beats "improve efficiency."

## Output Format

At the end of the process, present results in this structure:

1. **一句话购买理由** (One-sentence purchase reason)
2. **三大核心买点** (Top 3 buying points, ranked)
3. **信任证据** (Supporting evidence for each)
4. **客户场景故事** (A short before/after scenario illustrating the value)
